---
name: Shelf marketplace direction
description: User-defined purpose, interaction constraints, and initial demo scope.
---

The product connects shop owners with people renting shelf space and sourcing products, including small-capital international retail participants. Token A represents rented shelf space; Token B represents a shelf with products.

**Why:** The user wants direct collaboration between producers, retail participants, and existing shop owners.

The user wants white backgrounds like Google or ChatGPT, predominantly pictures, at most three short text blocks, and one main action per screen. Owner setup must include business categories, country/location, and a spatial shop layout with selectable rentable bricks.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritizes an extremely seamless visual experience over dense pages or explanatory text.

**How to apply:** Keep new flows staged and visual; avoid turning the product into a conventional dense admin dashboard.

The user selected an interactive demo with simulated purchases for the first version, rather than real transactions or blockchain.

**Why:** This was the user's explicit choice after discussing token, payment, and compliance implications.

**How to apply:** Clearly distinguish simulated trades, earnings, and illustrative product checks from real transactions or legal compliance clearance. Revisit scope before adding real payments.
